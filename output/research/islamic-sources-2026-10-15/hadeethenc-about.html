Project of Prophetic Hadith Encyclopedia and Its Translations:

An integrated project of selecting frequently repeated Prophetic Hadiths in the Islamic content, explaining them in a simplified and adequate way, presenting high quality translation based on accurate procedures into famous languages, then providing them for free through all available means..

Goals:

  1. Providing a developed, authentic, free, international reference for the Prophetic hadith translations..
  2. Providing an electronic memory of Hadith translations for translators while translating.
  3. Providing translations to the target audience through all available means.

Encyclopedia Advantages:

  1. Inclusiveness.
  2. Free.
  3. Variety of Translations.
  4. Continuous development.
  5. Precision.

Stages of Development:

  1. Building the encyclopedia in Arabic.
  2. Translating the encyclopedia into languages.
  3. Publishing and making the encyclopedia available online.
  4. Continuous development of the encyclopedia and its translations.
